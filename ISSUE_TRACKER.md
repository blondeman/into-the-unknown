## IDEAS

### Endless Story

* The snake has found a new way to regain its tail through eating ancient creatures
* The creatures will scurry around and when the snake eats them it grows longer
* The player can combine creatures into upgrades to the snake

### Difficulty

* Snake length - Increases chance to get hit
* Stage size - Increases number of rooms generated
* Stronger enemies - Enemies that take multiple hits and deal more damage
* Room theme - changes every few levels, looping upon completion

## Item Effects

* Mouth size - Makes hitting enemies easier
* Dash Meter - Dash is on a cooldown which can be decreased
* Dash invulnerability - Makes you invulnerable to damage while dashing
* Dash charges - Increase the number of consecutive dashes you can perform and store
* Scale armor - Decrease the amount of damage you take per hit
* Flame breath - Turn your dash into a flame spit attack which deals damage over time. Eating a burning enemy heals you slightly
* Handling - Increase your turn speed so moving and eating enemies is easier
* Blood thristy - Fire damages you but eating heals you
